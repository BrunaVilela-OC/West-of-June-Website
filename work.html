Borderland: Narcosis

Borderland started as a broad show about the Southern border crisis, but the audience made it clear that organized crime was what they wanted more of. After those episodes became clear outliers, I began shifting our editorial strategy toward cartels, organized crime, and the geopolitics surrounding them.

I eventually pitched Narcosis as a mini-series. The idea ultimately became a full rebrand of the show, which I continued to run as Creative Producer. Narcosis is still the franchise today.

Behind the story

What does China have to do with Mexican cartels?

While studying our audience, I noticed something interesting: viewers were responding to stories connecting China, organized crime, and fentanyl.

That got me curious about the origins of that connection. I developed an episode around the question, identified former DEA chief Ray Donovan as the right person to help unpack it, and wrote the interview questions.

Sometimes the best story ideas start with a really good question.

  • Led the show from development through publishing

  • Collaborated on episode packaging and distribution

Change Agents

Change Agents was IRONCLAD’s flagship show and the production I ran most consistently, producing it weekly from 2023 until I left in 2026. I owned the backbone of the show: host coordination, production lineup, recording logistics, post workflow, sponsor integrations, deliverables, final QC, and publishing.

Over time, my role expanded into guest research, rundowns, recordings, and closer editorial collaboration with host Andy Stumpf. I also oversaw the post-to-publish pipeline for larger productions like the Black Project mini-series and travel episodes, and brought in one of the show’s long-running presenting sponsors.
